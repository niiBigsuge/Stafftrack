# Project Idea: Corporate Staff Attendance and Punctuality Management System

I want to build a Corporate Staff Attendance and Punctuality Management System. It would be a web-based application that helps a company monitor daily staff attendance, enforce reporting times, measure punctuality, and maintain reliable historical records for management and Human Resources (HR).

The idea is not just to let staff sign in. It is to build a system that gives management a clear picture of who reported to work, who arrived late, who was absent, and how each department performs over time.
